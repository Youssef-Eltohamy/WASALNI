## 🇪🇬 النسخة العربية

### 1. عن المنتج
**وصلني** تطبيق موبايل = **منصة اكتشاف محلي** لربط سكان القرى المصرية بالمحلات، الخدمات، الحرفيين، و وسائل النقل القريبة منهم. الجوهر: **اكتشاف + تواصل مباشر + ثقة**. (مش متجر إلكتروني — التواصل بيتم عبر واتساب/اتصال مباشر).

### 2. معنى الاسم
"**وصلني**" = "وصّلني بـ..." / "خلّيني أوصل لـ..." — فكرة **الربط و الوصول**. الاسم بيوحي بالتواصل و توصيل الناس ببعضها و بالخدمات.

### 3. الجمهور المستهدف
- سكان قرى مصرية (نبدأ بكفر المقدام، الدقهلية).
- أعمار متنوعة (شباب لكبار سن)، **إلمام تقني متفاوت**، ٢٥٪ منهم أميّون.
- أجهزة أندرويد متوسطة، شاشات مختلفة.
- **النتيجة:** الشعار لازم يكون **بسيط جداً، واضح، يتعرّف عليه بسهولة، يشتغل صغير (أيقونة تطبيق) و كبير**.

### 4. شخصية العلامة (Brand Personality)
- **موثوق** (أهم صفة — الجمهور حذر من التطبيقات الجديدة)
- **عصري** بس **مش بارد** — احترافي و دافي فنفس الوقت
- **محلي** — بيخدم القرية، مش "كل مصر"
- **بسيط و واضح**

## 🇬🇧 English Version (for English-based models)

### 1. Product
**WASALNI** (Arabic: وصلني) is a mobile app = a **hyperlocal discovery platform** connecting rural Egyptian village residents with nearby shops, services, craftsmen, and transport. Core: **discovery + direct contact + trust**. Not e-commerce — contact happens via WhatsApp/phone.

### 2. Name meaning
"Wasalni" means **"connect me to..." / "get me to..."** — the idea of **linking and reaching**. It evokes connection and bringing people together with services.

### 3. Target audience
- Rural Egyptian villagers (starting with Kafr El-Maqdam, Dakahlia).
- Mixed ages, **varying tech literacy, ~25% illiterate**.
- Mid-range Android phones.
- **Therefore:** the logo must be **very simple, clear, instantly recognizable, work at small (app icon) and large sizes**.

### 4. Brand personality
- **Trustworthy** (most important — audience is wary of new apps)
- **Modern but warm** (professional yet approachable)
- **Local** (serves the village, not "all of Egypt")
- **Simple & clear**

### 5. Locked visual direction (do NOT change)
- **Palette: "Petrol Blue & Coral"**
  - Petrol Blue (primary): `#0D5C75`
  - Deep Navy: `#082F3D`
  - Coral (accent): `#FF7A45`
  - Light BG: `#F4F8FA`
  - Dark text: `#15252E`
- **Font: Cairo** (Arabic Google Font)
- **Vibe:** modern + trustworthy + warm

### 6. The 12 concepts already explored
*(So you know what's been tried; #1 is the current favorite)*

1. ⭐ **The Connection (favorite):** Two dots (one petrol, one coral) joined by a dashed arc. Symbolizes "we connect you to..."
2. Solid Connection: same but solid arc.
3. Straight Connection: two dots joined by a straight diagonal line.
4. Network/Hub: a central dot connected to two dots.
5. Signal: a dot emitting waves (wifi-like) — reach/broadcast.
6. Trust Pin: a map pin (📍) with a checkmark inside — "local" + "trusted".
7. Clean Pin: simple map pin with a coral center dot.
8. Pin + Chat: a map pin merged with a speech bubble — "location" + "communication".
9. Two Bubbles: two connected speech bubbles (petrol + coral) — two-way contact.
10. Nav Arrow: a navigation/direction arrow (Google-Maps style).
11. Locate: a GPS locate circle with a coral center dot.
12. Letter Waw: the Arabic letter "و" in Cairo font + a small coral dot.

### 7. What we want from you (pick one)
**(A) Evaluate:** Rank the 12 concepts best-to-worst and explain. Which is strongest as a trustworthy + simple + recognizable identity?

**(B) Propose:** Suggest **3-5 new concepts** better than the 12, using the same palette and vibe. Describe each in detail (shape, symbolism, how it looks as an app icon).

### 8. Constraints
- Must work as an **app icon** (rounded square) AND a **horizontal logo** (with the word "وصلني").
- **Simple** — recognizable even by an illiterate or elderly person.
- Use the locked palette (petrol blue + coral).
- Preferably symbolizes **connection / reach / location / communication** (meaningful, not abstract-for-its-own-sake).
- Avoid complexity/fine detail (will disappear at small sizes).
